About the Evening

Join us at Snipes Farm for an evening of dinner in the meadow and live music in the pavilion. A Night to Nourish is co-hosted by three nonprofits to raise money for hunger relief in Lower Bucks County.

This is a BYOB picnic: bring your own dinner, drinks, plates, flatware and napkins. Prefer not to cook? Order a boxed dinner from Ardana in Newtown when you buy tickets. Meals are picked up at the event and served cold.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get tickets?

Buy tickets by credit card from the Tickets link in the menu. To pay by check, Venmo or PayPal, email keller@pennsburykids.org.

Are kids included?

This is an adults-only event.

What time and where should I arrive?

Please arrive by 5:30 PM for the farm tour, dinner set-up and live music. Dinner begins at 6:30 PM and the evening wraps up around 8:30 PM. Snipes Farm, 890 W. Bridge Street, Morrisville, PA 19067.

What should I wear?

Smart casual. Choose shoes that work on a hay-covered barn floor, and bring outdoor shoes for the farm tour if it has rained recently.

What is included in the ticket price?

A beautiful evening on the farm with good company, live music and dancing. All ticket proceeds support local families who need food assistance.

Can I reserve my space or table?

A table of 6 is held for each ticket buyer. If you don't have a full table, arrive early to sit together, or contact Keller to hold space.

Do I need to bring anything?

Bring your own picnic dinner, drinks (beer and wine are welcome), plates, flatware and napkins. Coolers and food deliveries are fine. Boxed dinners are pre-order only.

Where do I park?

Snipes Farm has plenty of parking, and volunteers will direct you.

What if I need help getting to the registration area and tables?

A golf cart runs from the parking lot. Let a volunteer know, or contact Denise Smith at (215) 601-9426 or denises@snipesfarm.org, ideally one to two weeks before the event.

Can I get a refund if I can't attend?

Tickets are non-refundable, rain or shine, and count as tax-deductible donations to the host organizations. To transfer your ticket, email keller@pennsburykids.org with the new guest's name.

What if I can't or don't want to pay by credit card?

Make checks payable to Pennsbury Kids and mail them to 2122 N Crescent Blvd, Yardley, PA 19067, including guest names and meal choices. Venmo (@pennsburykids) and PayPal (keller@pennsburykids.org) are also accepted.

What happens if it rains?

The event is rain or shine. If conditions become dangerous, the event will be canceled and ticket sales will be treated as donations. No refunds will be given.

Who do I contact with a question not listed here?

Keller Arnold, (908) 812-9128 or keller@pennsburykids.org.

What meals can we purchase?

Ardana in Newtown prepares boxed dinners for $35 each (fees included), ordered through the Tickets page and picked up at the event. Each comes with cookies for dessert:

  • Pasta Salad with Salmon
  • Falafel Power Bowl Salad
  • Chopped Chicken Salad
